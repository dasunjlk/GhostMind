GhostMind v1.1.0 — choose your AI provider, smarter answers, easier updates.

### What's New

- Use any AI provider: add a Base URL and your API key (Groq, OpenRouter, and
  similar services work). GhostMind checks your details and shows the models
  you can pick from — no more being locked to one service.
- Smarter screen reading: GhostMind now finds the actual question on your
  screen before answering, so replies match what you're looking at.
- Steady answers: the same question now gives the same answer.
- Session memory: while GhostMind is open it remembers your earlier questions
  and answers. Close the app and the memory is wiped — nothing is saved.
- Rapid questions just work: ask one after another and each gets its own
  answer, in order.
- Updates made easy: new versions appear as a small banner inside the app and
  download themselves in the background. When it's ready, one click installs.
- A guided tour: the first launch walks you through every setting, step by
  step. Replay it anytime from Settings → About.
- Send feedback: tell us what you love or what's broken, right from the app.
  It's anonymous, privacy-safe, and helps us fix things faster.
- Fresh look: a new app icon, a friendlier branded installer, and a smaller
  download with the OCR engine included — nothing else to install.

### Install

- Installer: `GhostMind-Setup-1.1.0.exe` — per-user install
  (`%LOCALAPPDATA%\GhostMind`), no admin rights required.
- Portable: `GhostMind-Portable-1.1.0.zip` — extract and run `ghostmind.exe`.
- Verify downloads against `SHA256SUMS.txt`.

The installer is unsigned, so SmartScreen may warn - choose More info → Run anyway.
